# Master Prompt & System Instructions

## 🎨 DESIGN DIRECTION (MOST IMPORTANT)
- **Award-Winning Aesthetic**: Forget traditional real estate websites. Design this website like an award-winning Awwwards / Apple / Framer / Cuberto / Porsche / Sotheby's Realty website.
- **Immediate First Impression**: The experience should immediately impress the visitor within the first 5 seconds. The website must feel like a luxury architectural showroom rather than a property listing portal.
- **Design Inspiration**: Combine aesthetics from Apple, Framer, Cuberto, Awwwards, Porsche, Bentley, Sotheby's Realty, and The Local Project. Do NOT create a generic corporate website.
- **Prioritize Craft & Originality**: Do not prioritize speed of development or simplicity. Prioritize originality, visual impact, premium aesthetics, cinematic storytelling, and award-winning interaction design. Every section should have a unique layout. Avoid repeating the same card pattern throughout the website.

## 🎥 3D & CINEMATIC EXPERIENCE
- **Modern 3D Elements**: Include modern 3D interactive elements throughout the experience (e.g., interactive 3D luxury villa model, architectural models, glass spheres, golden rings, blueprint objects, architectural blocks).
- **Interactive Mouse Motion**: The 3D models and floating objects should rotate and react dynamically to mouse position and scroll velocity with smooth lighting and realistic glass/metallic materials.
- **Floating Interactive Cards**: Property cards must continuous float, tilt towards cursor (3D perspective), cast soft multi-layered shadows, scale smoothly, and glow subtly.
- **Scroll & Reveal Motion**: Section reveals using smooth parallax, upward title staggers, layer masks, depth transitions, and scale reveals with zero sudden pops.
- **Luxury Loading Experience**: Include a bespoke blueprint drawing / wireframe-to-solid golden line loading transition on initialization.

## 🌤 LIGHT LUXURY THEME ARCHITECTURE
- **Primary Background**: The website MUST use a luxurious warm light theme (`#FAF8F4` or `#FAFAF8`), with white and cream layers.
- **Dark Charcoal Usage**: Dark charcoal (`#111111`) MUST be used ONLY for selective premium highlight sections (e.g. "Why Choose Us" / selective CTAs). Do NOT make the entire website dark.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Bright, airy, and spacious editorial layout with high-contrast typography, generous negative space, and gold accents (`#C6A664`).

## ✨ MICRO-INTERACTIONS & HARDWARE-ACCELERATED ANIMATIONS
- **Interactive Details**: Include magnetic buttons, custom dynamic cursor glow/follower, glass backdrop blurs, shimmer effects, ripple feedback, and smooth 60 FPS spring animations.
- **Layout & Card Limits**: Avoid generic bootstrap cards and dense grids. Use maximum 4 feature items per block, 24px+ border radii, soft depth, and editorial typography.

## ❌ STRICT BANS
- ❌ No generic corporate or Bootstrap layouts
- ❌ No crowded dashboard grids or flat static cards
- ❌ No repetitive card templates
- ❌ No dark-mode only theme (must be warm light with selective dark accents)
- ❌ No sudden abrupt scroll reveals or unstyled default components
